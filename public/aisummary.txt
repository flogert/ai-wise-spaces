AI Wise Spaces is a web design and AI visibility company that helps local businesses become easier for AI assistants and search engines to understand, summarize, and recommend.

The company specializes in AI-optimized websites built with modern web technology such as Astro and Tailwind CSS. Services include AI-ready website development, local SEO, answer engine optimization, JSON-LD schema, llms.txt setup, robots.txt optimization, and migration from WordPress, Wix, and Squarespace.

AI Wise Spaces serves local businesses in Lancaster, York, Harrisburg, Reading, Allentown, West Chester, Fairfax, King of Prussia, Wilmington, and across the United States.

Ideal customers include plumbers, roofers, HVAC companies, electricians, gyms, salons, restaurants, coffee shops, yoga studios, photographers, landscapers, and other service businesses that want more leads from search and AI recommendations.
