# Marketing Strategy — AI Wise Spaces

## Objective

Generate qualified leads from local service businesses in Lancaster and nearby markets by:

- Driving targeted traffic
- Converting visitors into leads
- Positioning as the authority in "AI + local visibility"

---

## Core Positioning

We do NOT sell "AI websites"

We sell:
> More calls, more jobs, and better visibility on Google + AI tools

### Primary Value Proposition

- Get more local jobs without paying for ads
- Show up when people ask AI tools for recommendations
- Fix your website so it actually converts
- Help local businesses show up in Google and AI search

## Acquisition Channels

### 1. Google Search (SEO)

Create pages targeting:

/roofing-marketing-lancaster-pa  
/plumber-website-lancaster-pa  
/contractor-leads-pennsylvania  

Each page must include:
- Clear service + location
- FAQ section
- Simple explanations

---

### 2. Direct Outreach (Primary Early Growth)

#### Process

1. Search:
   - "roofers Lancaster PA"
   - "plumbers near me"
   - "med spa York PA"
   - "coffee shop Harrisburg PA"

2. Identify:
   - Poor websites
   - No clear messaging
   - No FAQ or structure

3. Send message:

"Hey — I checked your site and noticed it doesn’t show up well in Google or AI tools like ChatGPT.

I made a quick improvement example that could help you get more calls.

Want me to send it?"
